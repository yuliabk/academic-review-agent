# Scientific audit checks
- Central question and novelty: precisely bounded against actual literature.
- Study design: proper negative/positive/process-matched controls, experimental unit, randomization/blinding where applicable, n and independent replicates.
- Reproducibility: concentration, solvent, pH, temperature, duration, instrument configuration, preprocessing, exclusions, equations, statistical model.
- Quality: distribution rather than only mean where skewed; absolute vs normalized fluorescence; baseline and blank correction; carry-over and interference.
- Figures: legends, scale bars, units, color keys, panels, significance markers, representative vs quantified fields, raw-to-processed trace.
- Claims: association != causation; spectroscopy shifts not automatically binding mechanism; microscopy appearance not composition proof; electrostatic charge not standalone interaction proof; storage CFU not equal total live cell count.
- Confounds: surfactants, solvent residue, concentration differences, optical overlap, background fluorescence, method detection limits, batch effects.
- Consistency: exact values, abbreviations, species italics, sample designations and timepoints across abstract/results/figures/conclusions.
- Prioritize additional experiments by whether reviewer concern survives existing controls, and whether the additional result would alter conclusion.

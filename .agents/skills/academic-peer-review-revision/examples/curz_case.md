# Case study - CurZ / Mbbs (not a default research domain)
Published methodological lessons only: compositional microscopy claims require qualification; FTIR/fluorescence must be supported by process-matched controls and described as complementary; surfactants can confound electrostatic interpretations; wash-off, biological viability, and virulence are distinct endpoints; reviewer response claims require exact manuscript evidence and location verification.

## Avoid transplanting
Do not transfer source-specific numeric data, organism assumptions, experiment completion, manuscript identifiers or journal decisions into a different paper.
